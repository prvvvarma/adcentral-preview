Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 07 January 2026Last Updated: 07 January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Adora AI Solutions - FZCO (the "Company") collects, uses, discloses, transfers, and protects personal data when you access or use AdCentral ("AdCentral", "we", "us", or "our"), including its websites, web dashboards, applications, connected display devices, APIs, software, hardware integrations, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

AdCentral operates a global digital out-of-home (DOOH) advertising platform. Personal data may be processed, stored, and transferred across multiple jurisdictions. Where applicable data protection laws impose additional requirements, those requirements apply to users in the relevant jurisdiction.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that your personal data will be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, Adora AI Solutions - FZCO, incorporated in the United Arab Emirates, is the data controller responsible for determining how and why personal data is processed in connection with the AdCentral Platform.

Privacy contact: legal@adcentralglobal.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal data necessary to operate, secure, and improve the Platform. The categories of data collected depend on your role and interaction with AdCentral.

a) Advertisers

  • Business and company information
  • Authorized representative contact details
  • Billing, invoicing, and payment-related information
  • Advertising creatives, campaign configurations, and targeting settings
  • Campaign delivery, verification, and performance analytics

b) Device Owners and Venue Operators

  • Business and company information
  • Authorized representative contact details
  • Billing, invoicing, and payment-related information
  • Account and contact information
  • Registered device details (screen type, orientation, operating system, and technical identifiers)
  • Screen or venue location data at a placement or venue level (not continuous tracking of individuals)
  • Availability schedules, pricing configurations, and reporting preferences
  • Installation, verification, and compliance-related data

c) Platform Users (General)

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Session data, interaction logs, and usage analytics
  • Cookies and similar technology data

d) Employees and Contractors

  • Authentication credentials
  • Role-based access permissions
  • Operational logs and audit trails required for security, integrity, and compliance

AdCentral does not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of eighteen (18).

3. Web3 and Wallet Data

Certain Platform activities, including advertising spend information, delivery records, and verification-related metadata, are recorded on a blockchain as part of AdCentral's platform architecture to support transparency, auditability, and verification.

Where applicable, wallet addresses are treated as pseudonymous identifiers and are not directly associated with identifiable individuals unless such association is provided by the user or required by applicable law.

AdCentral does not custody, hold, or control any user digital assets, wallets, or private keys, and does not act as a custodian, broker, intermediary, fiduciary, or financial service provider in relation to digital assets.

Users remain solely responsible for the security and management of their wallets, credentials, and access mechanisms, where used.

AdCentral does not control, modify, reverse, or guarantee blockchain transactions. Information recorded on a blockchain may be publicly visible and immutable, and once recorded, cannot be altered or removed by AdCentral.

Users are not required to hold, manage, or transact digital assets, or to operate blockchain wallets, in order to access or use core Platform services, unless explicitly stated for a specific feature.

Blockchain-based components of the Platform may be subject to technical limitations, regulatory developments, or third-party network dependencies, which may affect availability or functionality.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Personal data is processed only where permitted by applicable law and based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract or steps taken at the user's request
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Legitimate business interests, including platform security, fraud prevention, analytics, service improvement, and network integrity
  • User consent, where required by law (which may be withdrawn at any time)

5. How We Use Personal Data

Personal data is used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the AdCentral Platform
  • Enable advertisers to create, manage, deliver, and measure campaigns
  • Allow device owners to register devices, configure availability, and access reporting
  • Verify device eligibility, placement accuracy, and compliance with content and display standards
  • Monitor device uptime, health, scheduling, and overall network performance
  • Process billing, payouts, invoicing, and financial records, including through third-party service providers
  • Conduct identity, business, or compliance verification where legally required
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, misuse, or security incidents
  • Improve functionality and user experience using aggregated or pseudonymized data
  • Communicate service updates, notices, and support-related messages

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

AdCentral does not sell or rent personal data.

Personal data may be disclosed only in the following circumstances:

  • To trusted service providers supporting hosting, analytics, payment processing, verification, customer support, and infrastructure, subject to confidentiality and data protection obligations
  • To legal or regulatory authorities where disclosure is required to comply with applicable law or lawful requests
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or similar corporate transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards

AdCentral does not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising where prohibited by law.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform uses cookies and similar technologies to support:

  • Essential functionality
  • Security and authentication
  • Session management
  • Performance and usage analytics

Where required by law, non-essential cookies are used only with user consent. Users may manage or disable cookies through browser or device settings. Disabling cookies may limit certain Platform features.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Monitoring, logging, and periodic security reviews

Security measures are implemented based on risk, industry standards, and evolving threats. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the Platform
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, and audit requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Retention periods vary depending on data type, purpose, and legal requirements. Data may be anonymized or securely deleted when no longer required.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of personal data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

Certain rights may be subject to limitations under applicable law.

Verified requests will be addressed within legally required timeframes.

Requests may be submitted to: legal@adcentralglobal.com

11. International Data Transfers

AdCentral operates globally. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are applied to protect such international transfers.

12. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for professional and commercial use only. AdCentral does not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under eighteen (18) years of age and will delete such data if identified.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in services, legal requirements, or security practices.

Material updates will be communicated through the Platform or other appropriate channels. Where required by law, additional consent will be obtained. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

14. Contact Us

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or AdCentral's data practices, please contact: legal@adcentralglobal.com
